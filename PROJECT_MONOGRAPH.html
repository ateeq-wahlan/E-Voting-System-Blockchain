ABSTRACT

Executive Technical Summary

Traditional paper-ballot and centralized electronic voting systems face persistent challenges regarding security, credibility, transparency, recount overhead, and single-point-of-failure vulnerabilities. While centralized database architectures require blind trust in the administering authority, our decentralized E-Voting System Using Blockchain Technology (2023–2024) manages the complete electoral lifecycle—from Form-6 voter biometric enrollment and Form-2A/2B party mandate management to encrypted vote casting, Proof-of-Work block mining, Form-7 electoral roll delisting, and real-time public auditing.

Voter identities are protected via HMAC-SHA256 Zero-Knowledge Nullifiers and Additive Homomorphic Commitments, ensuring complete ballot secrecy alongside public mathematical verifiability.

CHAPTERS 4 – 9 // ARCHITECTURE, RBAC GOVERNANCE & VERIFICATION SUITE

Electoral Governance Use Cases & Verification Matrix

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Separates Public Viewer / Voter Scope (masked contact/email PII, read-only ledger audit) from Electoral Commission Admin Authority Scope (authenticated via admin / admin).
  • Form-2A & Form-2B Party Mandate Governance: Allows EC Administrators to gazette new political party candidates (Form-2A) or revoke/deregister a party candidate from the active ballot (Form-2B under RP Act Sec. 37).
  • Form-6 Enrollment & Form-7 Electoral Roll Delisting: Enrolls voters with human facial portraits and 256-bit biometric signatures (Form-6) and supports statutory voter roll delisting (Form-7) while preserving on-chain immutability of already-mined anonymous ballots.
